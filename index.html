The curator

Meet Bub Pratt

poet · singer · songwriter · producer · music instructor

Before landing in Hilo, Bub spent 2003–2010 building creative spaces in Bremerton, Washington — founding and co-founding galleries, studios, and art installations that gave artists room to work loud.

He brought that same instinct to Kukuau Studio: one room where a beginner's first chord, a songwriter's rough demo, and a Monday-night horn solo all belong. With 20+ years of guitar instruction behind him, he curates the studio the way he teaches — start with the goal, then build the path.
